# MealForge privacy policy

MealForge does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- The app requests no internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
- Everything you enter (recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, pantry, spending, household details) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
- Android's own device backup may include MealForge's database if you have Android backup turned on; that is controlled by your Google account settings.
- Data leaves the app only when you choose to: sharing a grocery list, or exporting a backup file to a location you pick.
- Recipe photos are referenced from your device and are never copied or uploaded.

Nutrition and allergen information is approximate and is not medical advice.
